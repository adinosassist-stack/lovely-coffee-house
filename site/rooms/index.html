GOOD TO KNOW

Before you stay.

Lovely Rooms keeps rates and booking steps clear before you send an availability request.

01What are the room rates?+

Standard Rooms are P300 per night and the Executive Room is P450 per night. These are room-only rates and remain subject to availability.

02Is breakfast included?+

No. Breakfast is optional and separately priced. Continental is P50, Lovely Full is P70 and Executive is P95. A P45 coffee-and-muffin guest offer is also available for Standard Room guests.

03How many rooms are available?+

Lovely Rooms has three rooms in total: two Standard Rooms and one Executive Room.

04How do I confirm a booking?+

Choose your room and dates, send the availability request on WhatsApp, then wait for availability and payment instructions before treating the booking as final.
